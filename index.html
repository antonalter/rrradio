    Questions

    Is it really free?

    Yes. The seven live stations and the extension are free. Depot is optional support with extra comforts.

    Where does the music come from?

    The extension plays from YouTube in your own browser, with YouTube's own ads. The live stations play a hand-picked selection. rrradio does not sell music.

    Do I need an account?

    No. Your taste stays in your browser. Saving to Spotify or Apple Music uses the sites you are already signed in to.

    Phone?

    The live stations work in any phone browser; add the page to your home screen and it opens like an app. The personal radio needs a desktop browser for now.
