      Questions

      Is it really free?

      Yes. The seven live stations and the extension are free for good. Deepot is optional, for people who want to go all the way.

      Why can't I skip on the live radio?

      Because it's radio: one feed for everyone, picked by hand. Skips, hearts and your own taste live in the free extension.

      Where does the music come from?

      The extension plays from YouTube in your own browser. The live stations play a hand-picked selection. rrradio does not sell music.

      Ads?

      rrradio never adds ads of its own. The personal radio plays through YouTube, so YouTube decides what ads you see; with YouTube Premium there are none.

      How does paying work?

      Through Lemon Squeezy, which handles cards, PayPal and VAT. You get a key by email; paste it into the extension once. Cancel from the receipt, refund within 14 days.

      I can't afford it.

      Write to and we'll send a key. We mean it.

      Do I need an account?

      No. Your taste stays in your browser.

      Phone?

      The live stations work in any phone browser; add the page to your home screen and it opens like an app. The personal radio needs a desktop browser for now.
