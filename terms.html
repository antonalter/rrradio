What rrradio is

rrradio is a non-profit, volunteer-run music discovery project: a live radio page and a free browser extension. It recommends music and helps you listen to it through services you already use. It does not sell, license or host music for you.

Where the music comes from

  • The extension plays music from YouTube inside your own browser, through YouTube's own site and player, including YouTube's ads.
  • Saving tracks to Spotify, Apple Music or YouTube uses those services in your own browser, signed in as you. rrradio never sees or stores your passwords.
  • You are responsible for using each service in line with its own terms. rrradio is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by YouTube, Google, Spotify, Apple, Last.fm, MusicBrainz or any artist or label.
  • Artist names, track titles, covers and biographies belong to their owners and are shown only to identify and describe the music.
