Privacy

Last updated: 8 November 2026.

The short version

No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking cookies. Your taste stays in your browser. We only touch personal data where something technically has to: to stream audio to you, to take a payment you chose to make, or to answer a message you sent.

Who is responsible

The controller under the GDPR is the operator named in the legal notice. Contact: the address in the legal notice.

Cookies and storage

We set no cookies. The site and the extension keep your own choices in your browser's local storage so they are still there next time; nothing in it is used to follow you, and nothing leaves your device. That is why there is no consent banner, only a short notice.

Transfers outside the EU

GitHub, Google, Cloudflare and Lemon Squeezy are based in the USA. Transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, and on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses otherwise.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to its use, take it with you, and withdraw any consent at any time. Write to the contact above; we answer within a month. You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the one where you live.

Children

rrradio is not aimed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. We will never start selling data or adding tracking; that would be a different product.
