Privacy

Last updated: November 2026.

The short version

No accounts, no tracking, no ads of our own. Your taste lives in your browser.

What stays on your device

  • Your likes, skips, taste profile, settings and history are stored in your browser's extension storage. We do not receive them.
  • Your Depot key, if you have one, is stored the same way and checked with the payment platform about once a week.

Who the extension talks to

  • YouTube, to find and play music; Last.fm, MusicBrainz, Wikipedia, iTunes and Discogs, to read public information about artists, tracks and covers.
  • Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube, only if you use Depot's save feature, in tabs signed in as you.
  • Gumroad (or the support platform you used), only to check a Depot key.
  • Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

The live radio page

The page loads fonts from Google Fonts and the stream from our radio server. The server keeps no listening history beyond a live count of current listeners.

Contact

Questions or removal requests: write to the contact listed on the support page.
